About PeopleHub

PeopleHub is a centralized enterprise platform developed for TVS Electronics to manage employees, projects, resource allocation, HR cost management, finance cost management, analytics, and user administration in one place.

The platform improves collaboration between HR, Finance, Project Managers, and Administrators by providing a single source of truth for employee information, project allocations, financial visibility, and business insights.
