About Brand Cost Management

At TVE Electronics, this platform is designed to manage employees and projects in a clear and straightforward way. It brings together employee details, project allocations, and cost insights in one place. HR can oversee employee costs, Finance can provide accurate expense data, and the business gains a transparent view of project profitability. With better visibility and control, we can work more efficiently and achieve stronger growth.
